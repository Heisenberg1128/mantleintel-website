How it works

AI understands. Policy authorises.

Mantle lets the task proceed while keeping deterministic organisational policy in control of data, model access, approvals and evidence.

  1. 01

    Understand the task

    Determine the user’s intended outcome and the information genuinely needed to complete it.

  2. 02

    Inspect data and context

    Evaluate prompts and documents for relevance, sensitivity and declared restrictions.

  3. 03

    Apply organisational policy

    Use deterministic organisation rules as the final authority over data and model access.

  4. 04

    Transform and minimise

    Keep, mask, remove or escalate information before it crosses the trusted boundary.

  5. 05

    Show the exact payload

    Let the user approve the precise outbound content—not a summary of it.

  6. 06

    Route to an approved model

    Send only approved context to a model eligible for the task and classification.

  7. 07

    Restore approved context

    Reintroduce approved masked values only where policy and the workflow permit.

  8. 08

    Retain evidence

    Record decisions, approvals, routing and permitted actions without keeping raw removed values.
