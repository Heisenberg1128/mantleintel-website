The operating gap

AI is already inside the organisation. Control usually isn’t.

Teams are already using AI to review documents, analyse information, draft, research and support decisions. The real problem is not adoption—it is governing the data, permissions, models and actions around that work.

Security should not require making AI useless.

Employee AI Workspace · Current product

Useful work, through a governed boundary.

  1. 01

    Understand the task

    Determine the user’s intended outcome and the information genuinely needed to complete it.

  2. 02

    Inspect data and context

    Evaluate prompts and documents for relevance, sensitivity and declared restrictions.

  3. 03

    Apply organisational policy

    Use deterministic organisation rules as the final authority over data and model access.

  4. 04

    Transform and minimise

    Keep, mask, remove or escalate information before it crosses the trusted boundary.

  5. 05

    Show the exact payload

    Let the user approve the precise outbound content—not a summary of it.

  6. 06

    Route to an approved model

    Send only approved context to a model eligible for the task and classification.

  7. 07

    Restore approved context

    Reintroduce approved masked values only where policy and the workflow permit.

  8. 08

    Retain evidence

    Record decisions, approvals, routing and permitted actions without keeping raw removed values.

Architectural principle

Governance should understand the work.

The same information can be necessary in one workflow and inappropriate in another. Mantle combines task understanding and semantic classification with deterministic policy, permissions, human review, model routing and audit evidence.

AI understands. Policy authorises.

Future direction · Agent Workrooms

From AI answers to AI actions.

Enterprise AI is moving from analysis and drafting toward retrieving information, updating systems and coordinating work. The scarce layer becomes context, authority, permissions and evidence.

Today: govern what AI can see and use. Tomorrow: govern what AI agents can do.Agent Workrooms are a future product direction—not a claim of production-ready autonomous capability.
